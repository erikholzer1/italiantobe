SUBJECT LINE

Name the rule or the office, not "Newsletter #14." People decide to open from three words.

ONE TOPIC PER ISSUE

Two-generation limit, or 1948 rule, or apostilles — not a digest. A digest reads like effort spent on volume, not on them.

WHY IT MATTERS

This is the paragraph that turns a law update into a reason to act. Never skip it for the update alone.

ONE LINK ONLY

A menu of links reads as marketing. One clear door — usually the assessment — reads as help.

Worked example

One issue, fully written

From — Erik Holzer <info@italiantobe.com>
Subject — Two-Generation Rule: What Actually Changed
Since the March 2025 reform, an Italian-born great-grandparent no longer carries a claim on its own — the ordinary route now stops two generations back from you. That single change is why most of the questions I get start with "my great-grandfather was born in Italy, does that still count." It usually still counts, just not the way it used to. If your Italian ancestor is a grandparent or closer, the reform doesn't touch your line at all. If it's a great-grandparent or further, the ordinary consulate route is closed, but two things can still be true: a parent who lived in Italy for two years before you were born reopens it, and a case already filed before the cutoff is grandfathered in regardless of degree. Worth fifteen minutes to check even if you assumed you were out: run the free eligibility check — it asks exactly these questions and tells you which side of the line you're on. Sending it

Cadence & mechanics

Rhythm

  • Every second Tuesday, late morning (Naples time) — consistent beats trust, more than any particular day does.
  • Skip an issue rather than pad one. Nothing to say is a valid week.
  • One topic. If two feel urgent, that's two issues, two weeks apart.

Where the list comes from

  • Export current subscribers any time from /admin/leads → "Download newsletter list."
  • Only marketing-consented rows are in that file — everyone on it already opted in.
  • Each row includes its own unsubscribe_url column — merge that into the footer link per recipient, don't reuse one link for everyone.
  • Send from the same info@italiantobe.com mailbox the app already uses, so replies land somewhere you'll see them.

Topic bank — never run dry

  • One gate from the engine, explained in plain language (two-generation limit, 1948 rule, renunciation, marriage-loss).
  • One office/document deep-dive (apostille vs. no apostille, what a comune actually wants).
  • One "story from a case file" — anonymized, what looked closed and wasn't, or vice versa.

Format rules

  • Plain text, like the guide and confirmation emails already sent — no HTML template to break across inboxes.
  • Under 200 words in the body. If it needs more, it's two issues.
  • One link, never more than one.
